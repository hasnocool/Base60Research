## Acceptance
Every experiment has a falsifiable hypothesis, provenance, conventional baseline, appropriate nulls, primary metric, fixed seed, held-out validation plan, multiplicity policy and explicit negative-result reporting. AI never classifies findings. The deterministic adjudicator must reject missing or invalid evidence, explain every gate, and preserve an auditable record. No numerical coincidence is a discovery without independent replication.

## Acceptance
Every experiment has a falsifiable hypothesis, provenance, conventional baseline, appropriate nulls, primary metric, fixed seed, held-out validation plan, multiplicity policy and explicit negative-result reporting. No numerical coincidence is a discovery without independent replication. A base-60 result must beat relevant alternatives under equal-information comparisons, or be reported as having no demonstrated advantage.
